\chapter{Model Chain of Custody Form and Evidence Log}

The legal validity of a digital forensics report before a court of law or within corporate compliance audits rests on the unbroken documentation of the \textbf{chain of custody}. This record attests to who collected the evidence, on what date and under what conditions, how it was secured, who had access to it, and the mathematical algorithms certifying that it suffered zero alteration from initial seizure through courtroom exhibition.

This appendix provides a formal chain of custody model based on the guidelines of the international standard \textbf{ISO/IEC 27037:2012}.
